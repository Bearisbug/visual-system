先按 [STYLE.md](STYLE.md) 判断适用范围。以下是研究者编写的自然语言模板，未实测，非作者原始提示词。提交前替换所有字段；已有简报与用户选定方向优先。参考作者姓名只用于出处，不代替可观察的视觉描述。

## 新构思探索

```text
Design a distinctive logo for {{brand name}}, a {{specific internet, software, data or AI product}} for {{audience}}.
Its central product action or relationship is {{one concrete meaning}}. Explore how {{brand initial or abstract structure}} can express it through {{shared contour, modular assembly, layered planes or flowing bands}}.
Use the reference only for {{specific construction relationship}}. Create a new silhouette and internal structure for this brand. Avoid inheriting the reference brand's distinctive mark or lettering.
Keep {{shape family and complexity}} consistent. Begin with a clear flat structure, then apply {{limited palette or optional gradient}} if it improves the concept.
Prepare {{required symbol and wordmark arrangements}} on {{backgrounds}} and compare at {{actual target sizes}}.
Treat AI or technology as business context; choose imagery from the product brief rather than assuming a sparkle, brain, chip or infinity loop is required.
Show {{requested number of concepts}}; label the intended meaning and keep presentation decoration secondary.
```

## 已选版本定向修改

仅在已有自有品牌选定稿且用户要求局部调整时使用：

```text
Use {{approved own-brand artwork}} as the authority for {{silhouette, parts, letter sequence and composition}}.
Change only {{requested variable}} to achieve {{observable improvement}}.
Keep {{accepted decisions}} unchanged.
Use {{style reference}} only for {{specific permitted shape, spacing or expression property}}.
Show the result beside the approved version at {{comparison size}}.
If the request changes the concept, create a clearly separate concept instead of labeling it a minor revision.
```

## 输出核对

- 能说明候选图形与当前产品的关系；若没有可信的字母结合，允许采用独立抽象符号。
- 以当前目标尺寸查看符号、名称和开口；网站 favicon、应用图标与导航栏按实际投放场景验证，不假定一套裁切通用。
- 对比彩色、单色及深浅背景，确认重要部件仍可辨；出现合并时简化形状或制作有记录的小尺寸版本。
- 更换品牌后重新设计轮廓与结构，只保留参考的方法；不能只换名称、颜色或渐变。
- 对照输入图与实际结果，确认“保持”要求真的生效。
- 图中文字仅为探索参考；正式名称、字体与几何在可编辑母版中核对。
- 本模板没有工具专用参数；使用时按当前可用工具的真实接口提交。
